Reporting an issue?

A few details will help us understand what happened:

  • Your device model and iOS or Android version.
  • The version of Bible: Lessons & Quiz and your selected app language.
  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • The section, lesson, or question involved, if relevant.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.

Please leave out passwords and sensitive personal information. If you send a screenshot, hide anything you don’t want to share.

Privacy and data requests

For privacy questions or to request deletion of data you have shared with us, email softmalochka@gmail.com with “Bible: Lessons & Quiz Privacy” as the subject. Describe your request and, if it concerns a previous message, the email address you used to contact us.
