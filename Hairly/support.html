Reporting an issue?

A few details will help us understand what happened:

  • Your device model and iOS or Android version.
  • The Hairly app version.
  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.

Please leave out passwords and sensitive personal information. If you send a screenshot, hide anything you don’t want to share.
